Studio Ethos

Architecture Interiors Photography Product

NAVYAM is a multidisciplinary design studio founded by a Lolitha Santoshini, Satwika Neela, Ankitha Bejgam, driven by a shared belief in creating thoughtful, meaningful spaces. We work at the intersection of architecture, interior design, photography and experiences, blending creativity with a deep respect for context, people and place. Our process is collaborative and hands-on, combining research, design and storytelling to craft spaces that feel lived in, not just built.



At Navyam, we don't believe in a prescribed way of designing. Every project has its own language, character, and set of possibilities. Our approach is to listen before we intervene to understand the site, its context, its constraints, and even its imperfections. We work with what exists rather than working against it. We observe, interpret, experiment, and adapt allowing materials, surroundings, function, and circumstance to influence the direction of the design. Sometimes, the most unexpected ideas emerge from the most ordinary conditions. We don't begin with a solution and force it onto a space. We allow the space to inform the idea. For us, design is an act of response rather than imposition finding what is already possible, uncovering what is overlooked, and giving each space its due.

Design Philosophy

OUR APPROACH

01 / NEWNESS

We reject the dogma of repetitive form. Newness arises from unexpected material dialogues pairing low-carbon rammed earth with ultra-refined bronze, or regenerative bio-timber with hydro-thermal glazing systems.

02 / INTUITIVE

Spaces should be comprehended effortlessly through the senses before they are analyzed intellectually. We study human gait, natural light ingress, acoustic resonance, and tactile transitions to create effortless emotional resonance.

03 / ADAPTABILITY

Living architecture must respond to changing micro-climates and generational shifts. Our plans incorporate flexible structural envelopes, passive solar breathing corridors, and reconfigurable partition frameworks.
